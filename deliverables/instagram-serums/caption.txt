Five viral K-beauty serums, five completely different jobs.

You do not need all of them. The useful question is not “Which one has the most hype?” but “What is missing from my routine?”

💧 Tight, dehydrated skin — Torriden DIVE-IN Serum
✨ A fast dewy finish before makeup — Anua PDRN Hyaluronic Acid Capsule 100 Serum
🌿 A lingering post-breakout mark — Mediheal Madecassoside Blemish Repair Serum
🫧 A weightless first layer — COSRX The 6 Peptide Skin Booster Serum
🌙 Texture, mild breakouts, and a first retinol — innisfree Retinol Cica Moisture Recovery Serum

If two sound right, begin with the gentler hydrating option. Use it consistently before adding anything else. Introduce one new product at a time, patch test, and finish daytime routines with sunscreen.
