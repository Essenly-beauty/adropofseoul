Seoul transport is easy after one decision: pay per ride with T-money, or buy a short-term Climate Card for covered Seoul transit. The wrong choice is rarely disastrous; the bigger mistake is assuming every regional line or airport journey is included.

## Quick answer

Choose **T-money** for maximum flexibility, especially if you will travel outside Seoul or use airport services. Consider a **1-, 2-, 3-, 5- or 7-day Climate Card tourist pass** if your days stay inside its service area and you will ride frequently.

As of March 17, 2026, Seoul says new machines at many Lines 1–8 stations accept internationally issued cards for tourist Climate Card purchases and recharging; a service fee may apply. Coverage and sales points still require checking.

| Choose | When it works best | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|
| T-money | Mixed Seoul, airport and regional travel | Pay per ride; requires stored balance |
| Climate Card tourist pass | Frequent rides within covered Seoul services | Not every metropolitan line or airport journey is covered |
| Single-journey ticket | One exceptional subway ride | Subway only; inconvenient for transfers to buses |

## The tap that matters

Tap when entering and leaving the subway. On buses, board at the front, tap, then tap again before exiting through the rear. The exit tap calculates fares and preserves transfer treatment. Repeated failure to tap off can cause problems on pass products.

## T-money

T-money is stored value rather than unlimited travel. It remains the least complicated choice for mixed itineraries. Purchase and refund rules depend on the card and outlet; do not load the entire trip budget on day one.

## Climate Card

Short-term passes can be good value, but they do not mean “every train in greater Seoul.” Certain lines and journeys outside the service boundary are excluded. Airport and intercity services need special attention. Use Seoul's official coverage checker before buying.

## Navigation

Use Naver Map for the route and pay attention to the suggested subway car and exit. A station can have ten or more exits; choosing the right one often saves more time than choosing between two similar train routes.

## Last trains and accessibility

Last-train times depend on line, direction and transfer station. Check the complete route late at night. Elevators exist widely but may sit far from the exit your app recommends; use station accessibility information when stairs are a problem.

## FAQ

### Should tourists buy T-money or a Climate Card?

Buy T-money for flexibility across mixed routes. Consider a short-term Climate Card when you will take frequent covered buses and subways inside Seoul. Check the official service area before deciding.

### Can foreign credit cards buy a Seoul Climate Card?

Seoul introduced international-card payment at hundreds of new Lines 1–8 ticket machines on March 17, 2026 for tourist Climate Card products and single-journey tickets. Availability and fees can vary by machine and product.

### Do I tap off Seoul buses?

Yes. Tap when boarding and again before exiting. The second tap calculates the trip correctly and preserves transfer treatment.

### Does the Climate Card cover the airport train?

Do not assume that it does. Airport services and out-of-area segments have product-specific exclusions. Check the current official coverage for the exact airport route.

### Can I use Apple Pay directly on Seoul transit?

Do not rely on an overseas wallet as your only transit payment. Seoul's official public-transport guide has historically distinguished supported Korean mobile transit setups from Apple Pay. Carry a physical transit or payment backup.
