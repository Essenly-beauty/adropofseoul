The fastest way to make Seoul exhausting is to treat every famous pin as if it belongs to the same city centre. It does not. The river divides the map, the hills make short distances deceptive, and changing neighborhoods can consume the hour you meant to spend enjoying one.

This plan gives each day one clear mood. Swap days for weather and museum closures; keep each day intact.

## Quick answer

Use **Jongno or Myeongdong as a first-trip base** if you want the simplest access to the central days. Expect plenty of walking. Save places in Naver Map before leaving the hotel, and check each institution's current opening day.

## Day one: Old Seoul

Begin at Gyeongbokgung, then move west for Seochon and Tongin Market before returning toward Insadong and Ikseon-dong. Do not follow the old advice to visit Bukchon before breakfast: tourists may enter the designated red zone only between 10am and 5pm. Treat Bukchon as an optional, respectful detour within those hours, not a photo set.

[Follow the complete Old Seoul route](/articles/one-perfect-day-old-seoul).

## Day two: Seoul Forest and Seongsu

Start outdoors while your energy is high. Enter Seongsu after lunch, when more shops and cafés are open, and move in one direction toward Seongsu Station. Popups change too quickly for a permanent checklist, so use our verified place directory on the day.

[Follow the Seongsu route](/articles/seoul-forest-seongsu-walking-guide).

## Day three: Yeonnam and Hongdae

Use Hongik University Station as the anchor. Walk the Gyeongui Line Forest Park and Yeonnam side streets in daylight; cross into Hongdae after dinner when its main streets make more sense. This is a compact day, not a reason to add Gangnam.

[Follow the Yeonnam-to-Hongdae route](/articles/yeonnam-by-day-hongdae-by-night).

## Day four: Hannam, slowly

Start at Hangangjin, book the Leeum if the exhibition requires it, then use the galleries and shopping spine as an unhurried half-day. The neighborhood is steep and many cultural spaces close on Monday. End with dinner here or continue to Itaewon deliberately—do not pretend they are the same stop.

[Read our Hannam afternoon](/articles/hannam-afternoon-local-guide).

## Day five: Mangwon and the river

Buy lunch or picnic food around Mangwon Market, then walk to Mangwon Hangang Park. The park is about 1.5 kilometres from Mangwon Station, so the final approach is part of the day. Check rain, heat and air quality before committing to a long outdoor evening.

[Plan the picnic](/articles/han-river-picnic-like-a-local).

## What this plan leaves out

Gangnam, Namsan and major museums all deserve time, but not every first trip needs every district. Replace a day rather than stacking them. A good Seoul trip feels edited.

## How to adapt the plan

For **art and design**, replace Hongdae with COEX, Bongeunsa and Apgujeong. For **shopping**, replace the Hannam day with Myeongdong and Namsan. For **families or limited mobility**, shorten the outdoor walks and use one museum or mall as a stable anchor each day.

Do not assign fixed weekdays until checking closure calendars. Palaces, galleries and museums do not share one universal closed day.

## FAQ

### Is five days enough for Seoul?

Five days is enough for a strong first visit if each day stays geographically coherent. It is not enough to experience every major district, palace, museum and day trip without rushing.

### How many Seoul neighborhoods should I visit per day?

One main neighborhood plus an adjacent area is the practical limit for most travelers. Seoul Forest and Seongsu work together; Yeonnam and Hongdae work together. Jongno and Gangnam do not.

### Should I stay north or south of the Han River?

For this itinerary, stay north of the river because four of the five days centre on Jongno, Seongsu, Hongdae, Hannam and Mangwon. Choose the south side when clinics, Apgujeong, COEX or Jamsil dominate your trip.

### Do I need a Seoul transport pass for five days?

Not necessarily. T-money is flexible; the five-day Climate Card tourist pass may suit frequent travel within its coverage area. Compare your actual routes and check current exclusions before buying.

### Which day should change when it rains?

Move the Han River picnic first. Use a museum, COEX or a compact shopping district for heavy rain, then restore the river day when conditions improve.
