The prettiest week in Seoul is not a fixed appointment. Blossoms respond to weather, foliage shifts with temperature, and recent summers have made old averages feel less reliable. Choose a season by its tolerances, then check the forecast close to travel.

## Quick answer

For walking, **mid-spring and autumn** usually offer the easiest conditions. Visit in summer for long evenings and events only if you tolerate heat, humidity and heavy rain. Choose winter for lower outdoor crowds, clear air days and indoor culture—if proper cold does not ruin the trip for you.

| Season | Best for | Main trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | Flowers and mild walking days | Blossom crowds, variable air and rapid weather changes |
| Summer | Festivals and long evenings | Heat, humidity, heavy rain and possible typhoons |
| Autumn | Long walks and foliage | Popular weekends and variable foliage timing |
| Winter | Museums, cafés, baths and crisp city views | Real cold, short daylight and icy surfaces |

## Spring

Spring moves from cold, dusty days into mild walking weather and flowers. Cherry blossom forecasts are estimates, not guarantees; a warm spell or rain can compress the display. Early April is popular and accommodation demand reflects it. Later spring can be a calmer choice.

## Summer

Summer is hot and humid, with a rainy period commonly overlapping late June and July and possible typhoon impacts later in the season. Korea Tourism Organization advises checking real-time weather and warnings because patterns are changing. Plan outdoor stops early or late and keep an indoor alternative.

## Autumn

Autumn is the most forgiving season for long neighborhood walks. September can still feel summery; foliage generally belongs later, but exact timing varies by elevation and year. Festival weekends and perfect-weather Saturdays can be as crowded as spring.

## Winter

Winter is dry and genuinely cold. Snow in central Seoul is an event, not a daily background, so do not book expecting a white city. The reward is crisp architecture, heated interiors and a natural rhythm of museum, bathhouse, soup and café.

## The month matters less than the day

Check temperature, precipitation, wind, air quality and heat/cold alerts each morning. A rainy Seongsu day and a clear river day can be swapped without changing the trip.

## FAQ

### What is the best month to visit Seoul?

April, May, October and early November are popular for walking conditions, but no month guarantees perfect weather. Choose based on your tolerance for crowds, heat and cold, then check live forecasts.

### When do cherry blossoms bloom in Seoul?

Usually in spring, often around late March or early April, but the peak varies each year and can be shortened by wind or rain. Use an official seasonal forecast close to travel rather than booking around a fixed historic date.

### Is Seoul too hot in August?

August is commonly hot and humid, and heat alerts can limit long outdoor days. It remains visitable with early starts, indoor midday plans, hydration and flexible evenings.

### Does it rain all summer in Seoul?

No, but summer overlaps with a concentrated rainy period and possible intense downpours. Korea Tourism Organization advises checking real-time forecasts and weather alerts because patterns vary.

### Does it snow in Seoul in winter?

Snow occurs, but continuous postcard snow should not be expected. Winter planning should assume cold and potentially icy surfaces rather than guaranteed snowfall.
