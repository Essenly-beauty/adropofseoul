A Hangang picnic looks effortless once everyone is sitting down. The complicated part happens first: choosing the right park, carrying food from the station, finding a delivery rider and working out which convenience store has the ramyeon machine.

The easiest version is not to recreate every viral image at once. Choose one park, one way to eat and one reason to stay after sunset.

## Quick answer

For a first picnic, choose **Mangwon** if market food and sunset are the point, or **Yeouido** if easy subway access, delivery zones and park facilities matter more. Allow three to five hours and arrive in daylight.

| Picnic plan | Best park | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| Market food and sunset | Mangwon | Buy food before the walk to the river |
| Delivery chicken with easy access | Yeouido | Meet the rider at a designated delivery zone |
| Ramyeon and city lights | Yeouido or Ttukseom | Confirm that the park convenience store has a cooking machine |
| Bridge views and an evening walk | Banpo | Check fountain and event schedules |
| A quieter picnic | Ichon or Jamwon | Bring food with you; delivery is less straightforward |

## First, choose the park

The best Hangang park is usually the one that belongs to the rest of your day.

Choose **Mangwon** after Mangwon Market or an afternoon around Hongdae. The market solves the food question before you reach the river, although the official visitor guide places the park about 1.5 kilometres from Mangwon Station. This is a neighborhood evening, not a quick exit from the subway.

Choose **Yeouido** for the simplest first picnic. It has direct subway access, convenience stores and designated delivery points, but expect crowds on warm evenings and festival weekends.

Choose **Ttukseom** if you are already on the east side of Seoul. It combines broad river views with park facilities and official delivery zones.

Choose **Banpo** for bridge architecture and the Rainbow Fountain rather than for the easiest food logistics. Performances are seasonal and can change because of weather, maintenance or events.

Choose **Ichon or Jamwon** when a calmer atmosphere matters more than recreating the most famous version of a Hangang picnic. Bring food before entering the park instead of assuming delivery will be simple everywhere.

These parks are alternatives, not a sequence. Crossing Seoul to collect several versions of the same river evening is rarely worth it.

## Option one: bring food from the neighborhood

This is often the least stressful plan.

At Mangwon, buy kimbap, fried snacks, fruit or prepared dishes at Mangwon Market before walking to the river. Elsewhere, use a supermarket, bakery or restaurant near the station.

Buying food first avoids app registration, Korean-language phone calls and the problem of describing your location to a delivery rider. It also gives the picnic a stronger connection to the neighborhood instead of making the park feel like an isolated destination.

Do not overbuy. One substantial item, something fresh, water and a small treat are enough. A picnic becomes less enjoyable when half the evening is spent carrying containers and sorting leftovers.

## Option two: order delivery

Food delivery at the Hangang is real, but it does not mean a motorcycle will arrive beside your picnic mat.

Meet the rider at a designated **delivery zone**, or `배달존`. The system keeps motorcycles away from pedestrian areas and bicycle paths. Select the delivery-zone pin or address in the app, watch the order status and walk to the meeting point before the rider arrives.

As last checked in August 2026, Seoul listed six established delivery points across Yeouido, Ttukseom and Mangwon:

- Yeouido Underpass
- Entrance to Yeouido Parking Lot No. 2
- Entrance to Mulbit Square
- Ttukseom Information Center
- Ttukseom Underpass
- Mangwon Underpass

The city may add temporary zones for seasonal or nighttime programs. Promotions and temporary locations are not permanent park services, so use the current official notice rather than an old map screenshot.

The harder part for an international visitor may be the app itself. Registration, Korean phone-number verification, payment methods and communication with the rider vary by service. Multilingual signs and QR-linked guidance may help at selected zones, but should not be assumed at every park.

If the app blocks you at registration or payment, do not spend the sunset troubleshooting it. Buy food near the station or use a park convenience store instead.

## Option three: make Hangang ramyeon

“Hangang ramyeon” is ordinary instant ramyeon cooked in an extraordinary place.

Participating convenience stores sell noodles with a disposable cooking bowl designed for the machine. Choose the ramyeon first and follow the instructions on that store's cooker. The machine dispenses water and heats the bowl; this is different from bringing a stove or cooking equipment onto the grass.

![Several bowls of ramyeon cooking on automatic machines at a Hangang convenience store](https://yisdsaetxgrboqlirvgb.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/media/articles/han-river-ramyeon-machine.png)

*Participating convenience stores provide the cooking bowl and machine. Controls and equipment vary by location. Photo supplied to A Drop of Seoul.*

The controls vary, but the usual process is simple:

1. Buy a ramyeon packet and the correct cooking container.
2. Put the noodles and seasoning in the bowl.
3. Place the bowl on the machine and select the matching setting.
4. Add an egg or cheese near the end, if sold by the store.
5. Carry the bowl carefully. It will be extremely hot.

Do not assume every riverside convenience store has the same machine, ingredients or operating hours. Queues can also grow around sunset. If ramyeon is the main reason for your visit, find the store before choosing where to sit.

Ramyeon is the easiest spontaneous meal, but not necessarily a complete picnic. Kimbap, dumplings or a simple drink are more useful additions than several more cups of noodles.

## What about picnic rentals?

Private rental shops near popular parks may offer mats, small tables, chairs, blankets, lights or games. There is no single citywide rental system, so prices, identification requirements, deposits and return times vary.

Confirm four things before paying:

- What is included
- When the equipment must be returned
- Whether identification or a deposit is required
- What happens if it rains

A mat is enough for most visits. More equipment means more time collecting and returning it, especially when the shop is not directly beside the park entrance.

## Mats and shade tents are not the same

Picnic mats are the simple option. Shade tents and canopies follow separate seasonal rules and may be used only in designated areas.

For the 2026 operating season, Seoul's notice allows small shade structures in 15 designated areas from March through November. The stated limit is approximately 2 × 2 metres. Permitted hours are 9 a.m.–7 p.m. from March to May and September to November, and 9 a.m.–8 p.m. from June to August.

That means a shade tent cannot simply stay up throughout a late-night picnic. Read the signs at your park and check the current notice before renting one; zones, dates and operating rules can change.

## The rules that matter

A picnic is welcome. Treating the park like a private campsite is not.

Do not use portable burners, barbecues or open flames. Convenience-store ramyeon machines are installed food-preparation facilities, not permission to cook independently on the lawn.

Take responsibility for every container, cup and piece of packaging you bring. Sort waste at the marked stations and take it with you if the bins are full. Keep pedestrian and bicycle routes clear, and never ask a delivery rider to drive through the park to reach your mat.

Keep music and conversation at a level that does not take over the surrounding lawn. Follow posted alcohol controls and event restrictions; excessive drinking or behavior that disturbs other visitors can lead to enforcement.

## The weather rule

A Hangang picnic depends on more than whether it is raining.

Check wind, temperature, air quality and recent river conditions. Strong wind can lift a light mat or loose packaging, while summer heat can make uncovered food unsafe surprisingly quickly. Access to low-lying riverside areas may be restricted after heavy rain.

Arrive before sunset. Finding a delivery zone, convenience store, toilet and comfortable place to sit is much easier in daylight. Then stay through blue hour—the river lights are often the part worth waiting for.

## Our easiest first picnic

**Mangwon:** buy food at Mangwon Market, walk to the river, choose a spot after seeing the wind and crowds, and stay through blue hour.

**Yeouido:** buy ramyeon at a park convenience store or meet a delivery rider at an official delivery zone, then walk away from the busiest entrance before sitting down.

Neither requires a perfect setup. The point is not the equipment or even the menu. It is having somewhere comfortable to sit while Seoul changes from daylight to river lights.

## FAQ

### Can tourists order food delivery at the Hangang?

Sometimes, but the experience depends on the app. A Korean phone number, local registration or a supported payment method may be required. Use an official delivery zone and keep food near the station as your backup plan.

### Where do I meet the delivery rider?

At the delivery zone selected in the app, not at your picnic mat. Confirm the Korean place name and pin before ordering, then move to the meeting point before the rider arrives.

### Can I cook at Hangang Park?

Do not bring a portable burner, barbecue or open flame. Ramyeon machines at participating convenience stores are installed facilities and can be used according to the store's instructions.

### Which park is best for Hangang ramyeon?

Yeouido and Ttukseom are convenient starting points because they combine popular picnic areas with park facilities. Machine availability and store hours can change, so confirm the specific convenience store rather than choosing only by park name.

### Can I drink alcohol at a Hangang picnic?

Moderate drinking is commonly seen, but posted restrictions, special-event controls and rules against disruptive behavior still apply. Check the signs in the area where you sit.

### Can I put up a tent anywhere?

No. Shade tents are permitted only in designated zones, during specified months and hours, and within the current size limit. A picnic mat has fewer complications.
