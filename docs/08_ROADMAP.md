# 08 — Product and Technical Roadmap

**Owner:** CEO / CPO
**Purpose:** Preserve direction without expanding Phase 1 scope.

---

## Phase 1 — Trust and Profile Foundation

Build:

- editorial integration,
- Skin and Hair Profile,
- anonymous completion,
- result before signup,
- Beauty Passport,
- versioned history,
- explainable editorial recommendations,
- consent and analytics,
- My Seoul Drop gateway.

Do not build AI Concierge.

---

## Phase 1.5 — Better Personalization Operations

Possible additions after data quality is proven:

- editorial mapping admin tools,
- profile update reminders,
- improved recommendation reason UI,
- saved articles/products/places,
- profile change comparison,
- localization,
- lifecycle emails based on explicit consent,
- content quality and medical review workflow.

Entry criteria:

- reliable completion,
- low link failure,
- stable taxonomy,
- editorial operations owner,
- privacy review.

---

## Phase 2 — My Seoul Drop Utility

Potential scope:

- robust saved collections,
- product and place catalog,
- maps,
- Seoul neighborhood discovery,
- Olive Young discovery layer,
- user-created drops or itineraries,
- contextual recommendation filters,
- partner links with disclosure.

Booking remains a separate decision.

---

## Phase 3 — Beauty Intelligence Layer

Potential scope:

- richer knowledge graph,
- ingredient/product/place relationships,
- confidence-aware recommendations,
- longitudinal profile patterns,
- feedback loops,
- editorial evidence references,
- profile-aware search.

Guardrail:

- do not treat correlation as medical truth.

---

## Phase 4 — AI Beauty Concierge

Only after:

- profile taxonomy is stable,
- recommendation explanations are trusted,
- data consent is explicit,
- evaluation framework exists,
- human escalation and safety limits are designed,
- cost and latency are acceptable.

Potential capabilities:

- answer questions using the user's Passport,
- explain recommendations,
- compare routines and products,
- plan Seoul beauty discovery,
- summarize changes over time.

Not allowed:

- medical diagnosis,
- fabricated certainty,
- hidden sponsored ranking,
- unsupported treatment plans.

---

## Phase 5 — adropof Connection

adropof products may connect to the platform when:

- user need is validated,
- editorial independence is protected,
- disclosure is explicit,
- product claims meet the same evidence standards,
- own-brand ranking is not secretly privileged.

The platform should remain valuable without purchasing adropof.

---

## Decision gates

Before each phase approve:

- user problem evidence,
- trust and safety impact,
- data requirements,
- operational owner,
- technical readiness,
- monetization conflict,
- success metric,
- stop/rollback condition.
